Individual Project Site Plan

Development & Design Blueprint for LocalPulse

Site Name

LocalPulse

Selection Rationale: The name "LocalPulse" perfectly captures the core mission of the web application: keeping users instantly connected to the "pulse" or active heartbeat of professional tech gatherings, networking events, and career opportunities happening within their immediate geographic region.

Optional Domain Availability: localpulse-events.org

Site Purpose

LocalPulse serves as an interactive, API-driven hub designed to aggregate, filter, and display local tech networking events and professional meetups. By providing clean categorization, integrated maps, and calendar syncing, the application removes friction for developers, engineers, and digital professionals seeking to expand their physical networks, find community mentorship, or uncover localized career growth channels.

Target Audience Scenarios

To establish structural scope and determine primary user flows, the site platform architecture directly addresses the following user inquiries:

  • Scenario 1: "I am a junior software engineer who just moved into a new city. Where can I find a list of upcoming, free local Python or JavaScript developer meetups scheduled for this week?"
  • Scenario 2: "I want to host or find tech networking events nearby. Where can I filter events specifically by distance or view them on an interactive regional map?"

Color Scheme

The chosen visual palette leverages high-contrast tones to evoke a crisp, modern tech environment. Contrast metrics strictly meet WCAG AA standards to guarantee readability across all devices.

Primary (Slate Navy)

#0f172a

Applied to main semantic headers, typography headings, and heavy structural block backgrounds.

Secondary (Pulse Emerald)

#036c4e

Applied strictly to structural borders, hyperlinked items, interactive elements, and CTA callouts.

Neutral Light (Canvas)

#f8fafc

Applied to global site-wide background body tags and structural whitespace containers.

Neutral Dark (Charcoal)

#334155

Applied to standard body paragraphs and description text items to ensure structural eye comfort.

Typography

Aa Bb Cc Dd Ee Ff Gg Hh Ii Jj Kk Ll Mm Nn Oo Pp Qq Rr Ss Tt Uu Vv Ww Xx Yy Zz

Application: Leveraged across all standard body paragraphs, block lists, explanations, and footer notes to maintain peak legible readability.
